## Active Lane

`g02.001` is the live planning/control lane.

Its job is to carry the recovered overhaul posture into a fresh generation,
freeze the real active seams from evidence, and sequence the first bounded
normalization waves without collapsing them back into stale contraction-era
language.

`g02.002` is the first bounded execution wave.

Its job is to freeze the currently executing proof-app admin/media pattern
posture across Underlay, Poodle, Dairy, `acme-admin`, and `cp-admin` before
any broader consumer rollout resumes.

`g02.003` is the first downstream consumer gate.

Its job is to take the frozen proof-app admin/media/detail/list/dialog/form
patterns into `compli-me/admin` as the first honest downstream rollout target.
That gate is now complete enough to use as the downstream generalization
baseline.

`g02.004` is the next downstream consumer gate.

Its job is to take the completed `compli-me` generalization result into the
Songsprout family, keeping `greenhouse` and `bloom` bounded while leaving
`stem` out of the UI rollout scope. That gate is now complete enough to use as
the next downstream generalization baseline.

`g02.005` is the completed Composer downstream consumer gate.

Its job is to take the completed Songsprout result into
`loophole/composer/composer-admin`, keeping the first rollout slice bounded to
the standard admin CRUD families and scan-history browse shell while leaving
moderation, rules, and hardware workflows explicit local exceptions. That gate
is now complete enough to use as the final downstream generalization result.

`g02.006` is the completed closure lane.

Its job is to inventory the deferred workflow-local exceptions left open by the
Songsprout and Composer gates, confirm which should remain app-local versus
merit future shared-surface planning, and close the broad consumer-family
rollout line cleanly. That line is now closed.

`g02.007` is the current package-consolidation lane.

Its job is to migrate Underlay and the affected consumer families from the old
split Poodle package names to the consolidated `@poodle/svelte` package
surface without reopening the old broad rollout line.
