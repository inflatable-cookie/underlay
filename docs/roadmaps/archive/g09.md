## Intent and boundary

`g09` made Underlay a reference foundation with written, implemented,
and proved config, workspace, migration, transport, and testing
guarantees across the six-consumer family. Its config-convergence,
dependency, and elective-major work completed 2026-08-04 then continued
into consumer workspace normalization and contract fidelity. The
invalid empty-`g10` rollover was removed 2026-08-26 and its work
recovered into `g09.021`–`g09.045`; the 2026-08-27 cross-tab finding
added `g09.053` and renumbered the fleet closeout to `g09.054`.

## Canonical destinations

Config, workspace, migration, testing, bootstrap, runtime, access,
route, and release rules live in their `docs/contracts/` owners
(`023`, `024`, `027`–`029`, and the scan-policy/tooling surfaces).
Nothing in this roll-up is executable.

## Migrations, limits, and retained risks

Retired `db:*` selectors, fail-open local overlays, auth aliases, and
mixed batch-delete routes have no compatibility window. `TestDb`
remains the shared-crate/single-schema seam; multi-schema lifecycles
stay uncompiled. Advisory god-file and comment-ratio warnings were
kept advisory by operator choice.
