# g08 - Audit Remediation And Edge Hardening

Status: archived
Kind: roll-up
Generation: `g08` (32 roadmaps, `g08.001`–`g08.030` plus `g08.017b`)
Succeeded by: `g09`

## Intent and boundary

`g08` acted on the July 2026 deep audit: the core was strong and the
problems concentrated at the edges and in process. It closed the
XSS-to-takeover chain and trust-boundary gaps first, then correctness
bugs, then Rust structural seams and TypeScript surface debt, closing
on the versioning, i18n, and docs-weight posture decisions. Remediation
only; no new feature work.

## Shipped outcomes

- Lane A (security/edge): session-token exposure, editor preview
  sanitization, open-redirect guard, upload content-type/SVG/size
  enforcement, trusted-proxy IP resolution, error-header leak, CORS
  gating, distributed rate limiting, http-client SSRF/timeout defaults,
  auth hardening batch.
- Lane B (correctness/test gate): form-feedback clobber, dead login
  handler, media validation bypass, green gated unit pipeline.
- Lane C (Rust seams): error taxonomy, media domain-type relocation,
  pagination collision and the `underlay-query` split, auth-postgres
  adapter decision, postgres adapter integration tests, workspace
  dependency and lint hygiene.
- Lane D (TS surface): SSR-global guard, export-map diet, EntityList
  generics and split, strict-type and dependency hygiene.
- Lane E (posture): front-door repair, artifact cleanup, contract-sync
  decision, versioning story (`0.8.0`, `v0.8.0` tagged), English-only
  i18n decision in Contract `090`, archival docs-weight reduction
  (closed generations designated frozen archival; physical move
  deferred with rationale).

## Canonical destinations

Edge and auth rules live in Contracts `020`/`030`/`031`/`040` plus
guide `068`; envelopes, errors, media, and testing in their contracts;
versioning in Contract `023`; i18n posture in Contract `090`.
Nothing in this roll-up is executable.

## Migrations, limits, and retained risks

Consumer-visible changes (session shape, redirect helper, upload
enforcement, header removal, rate-limit config, error taxonomy,
media-type paths, pagination naming, export map) each landed under the
`023` rollout process with six-consumer proof.

## Deferred items

None. All 32 roadmaps are done; `v0.8.0` is tagged.
