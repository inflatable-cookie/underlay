## Intent and boundary

`g02` ran the Poodle-era consumer normalization and overhaul runway:
freeze the proof-app pattern, gate the first consumer, roll the admin
pattern out to Compli Me, Songsprout, and Loophole Composer, then close
with the deferred-exception freeze and the Poodle Svelte package
consolidation migration (`g02.007`). Its batch cards refined that lane
and were retained as generation-local history at closeout.

## Shipped outcomes

- Proof-app pattern freeze and first consumer gate.
- Consumer rollout gates for Compli Me, Songsprout, and Loophole
  Composer (since deleted as a live repository; see `g10`).
- Deferred-exception freeze and closure lane.
- Poodle Svelte package consolidation migration, with its
  consolidation-boundary card.

## Canonical destinations

Consumer pattern authority now lives in `docs/contracts/111-*`,
`docs/usage/templates/`, and the Poodle package. Nothing in this
roll-up is executable.

## Migrations, limits, and retained risks

None retained. The Loophole Composer repository was later removed from
live fleet authority (see `archive/g10.md`); frozen `g02` evidence
naming it is historical record.

## Deferred items

None. All `g02` roadmaps are complete.
